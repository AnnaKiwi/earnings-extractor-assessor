Patient case study: Deepening of response over time • Background: Male, 57 years old, RCC diagnosed 10 years ago, metastatic disease ~5 years ago • Prior therapy: 2 prior therapy lines cabozantinib + nivolumab (1 mo) → progression & lenvatinib + everolimus (3 yrs) → progression • Baseline: Two enlarged mediastinal lymph nodes both > 2 cm longest diameter multiple small lung and pancreas lesions membrane ENPP3+ <25% tumor cells • Response: PR week 6, deepened to CR week 42 normalization of lymph nodes disappearance of lung and pancreas lesions • Treatment and response ongoing at month 16 Baseline 15.5 Months
Conclusions • XmAb819 (ENPP3 x CD3) showed meaningful antitumor activity in patients with advanced ccRCC. • Antitumor activity was observed in a dose-dependent manner, with a cORR of 30% at 100 μg/kg, 44% at 200 μg/kg, and 45% at 400 μg/kg, and a majority of responses ongoing at data cut. • Consistent activity in subgroups with prior exposure to HIF2α inhibitors (cORR 35%) and ≥2 VEGFR-TKIs (cORR 45%). • These data support continued development of XmAb819 as a monotherapy for advanced clear cell RCC and evaluation of rational combination strategies in earlier lines of treatment. cORR, confirmed objective response rate; HIF2α, hypoxia-inducible factor 2-alpha; TKI, tyrosine kinase inhibitor; VEGF, vascular endothelial growth factor receptor Pal SK, et al.
