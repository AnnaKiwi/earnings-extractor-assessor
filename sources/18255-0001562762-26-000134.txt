CATO REPORTS 3Q RESULTS
CHARLOTTE, N.C. (November 19, 2026) – The Cato Corporation (NYSE: CATO) today reported a net loss of $10.4
million or ($0.54) per diluted share for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026, compared to a net loss of $5.2 million or
($0.28) per diluted share for the third quarter ended November 1, 2025.
Sales for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026 were $148.0 million, a decrease of 4% from sales of $153.7 million for
the third quarter ended November 1, 2025.
The Company’s same-store sales for the quarter decreased 1% compared to
2025.
For the nine months ended October 31, 2026, the Company reported net income of $0.1 million or $0.01 per diluted share,
compared to net income of $5.0 million or $0.25 per diluted share for the nine months ended November 1, 2025.
Sales for
the nine months ended October 31, 2026 were $481.3 million, a decrease of 3% to sales of $496.8 million for the nine
months ended November 1, 2025.
Year-to-date same-store sales decreased 0.6% compared to 2025.
“Although our overall sales trend improved compared to the second quarter, the effect of persistent inflation and higher
fuel costs continues to erode our customers’ discretionary income” stated John Cato, Chairman, President, and Chief
Executive Officer.
“The continued negative sales trend caused us to take more markdowns which impacted our gross
margins.
We believe the pressure on our customers’ discretionary spending will continue into the fourth quarter and the
foreseeable future.
We will continue to manage our expenses and inventory levels as we navigate through this
difficult
economic environment.”
Gross margin decreased from 32.0% to 30.0% of sales in the quarter due to lower merchandise margins and higher
markdowns, partially offset by lower buying and occupancy costs as a percent of sales.
SG&A expenses as a percent of
sales decreased from 37.1% to 37.0% of sales during the quarter primarily due to lower payroll, professional fees and
insurance costs as a percent of sales, partially offset by expenses related to store closings. SG&A expenses were $54.7
million, a $2.2 million reduction compared to last year’s third quarter. Tax expense for the quarter was $0.2 million versus
tax benefit of $1.2 million in the prior year, primarily due to a reduction in the release of reserves for uncertain tax
positions in the current year.
Year-to-date gross margin decreased to 33.5% of sales from 34.5% in the prior year primarily due to lower merchandise
margins and higher markdowns, partially offset by our tariff refund as well as lower buying and occupancy costs as a
percent of sales.
The year-to-date SG&A rate was 33.8% versus last year’s 34.2% primarily due to lower payroll and
insurance costs as a percent of sales.
Year-to-date SG&A expenses decreased $7.0 million to $162.7 million. Tax expense
for the nine-month period was $0.9 million compared to $0.5 million tax benefit last year, due to a reduction in the release
of reserves for uncertain tax positions in the current year.
Year-to-date, the Company has closed 22 stores and opened two stores.
The Company is in process of closing
approximately 90 additional stores in the fourth quarter.
As of October 31, 2026, the Company has 1,049 stores in 31
states, compared to 1,101 stores in 31 states as of November 1, 2025.
The Cato Corporation is a leading specialty retailer of value-priced fashion apparel and accessories operating three
concepts, “Cato,” “Versona” and “It’s Fashion.”
The Company’s Cato stores offer exclusive merchandise with fashion
and quality comparable to mall specialty stores at low prices every day.
The Company also offers exclusive merchandise
found in its Cato stores at www.catofashions.com.
Versona is a unique fashion destination offering apparel and
accessories including jewelry, handbags and shoes at exceptional prices every day.
Select Versona merchandise can also
be found at www.shopversona.com.
It’s Fashion offers fashion with a focus on the latest trendy styles for the entire
family at low prices every day.
