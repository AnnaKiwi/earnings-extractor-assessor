XTI Aerospace Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results

 

MIAMI, October 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- XTI Aerospace, Inc. (Nasdaq: XTIA) (“XTI Aerospace,” “XTI,” or the “Company”), an aerospace and advanced technology platform and parent company of Drone Nerds, LLC, (“Drone Nerds”), a leading drone solutions platform serving commercial, enterprise and government customers, today announced financial results for its second quarter ended June 30, 2026, and provided an update on the Company’s outlook for 2026.

 

2026 Second Quarter Highlights (Inpixon results excluded and reflected in discontinued operations):

 

●Revenue of $32.4 million

 

●Gross profit of $5.6 million

 

●Gross profit as a percentage of revenue of approximately 17%

 

“Demand for enterprise drone solutions continues to build across enterprise and government buyers and, as one of the nation’s leading enterprise drone providers, we believe we are well positioned to capitalize on that opportunity,” said Jeremy Schneiderman, XTI Aerospace’s Interim Chief Executive Officer. “My focus is on building a disciplined organization, spending carefully, and executing consistently. Importantly, the leadership team, sales organization and technical experts who built this business remain in place and are committed to supporting our customers across public safety, government, energy, infrastructure, agriculture and commercial markets.”

 

“While we are navigating a dynamic regulatory environment, including Section 232 tariffs that took effect on September 3 and other pending drone-related regulations, our team remains focused on accelerating growth by strengthening our product offerings, increasing our marketing investments, and maintaining disciplined expense management. This is an exciting new chapter for the Company and while we are taking a prudent view towards our full-year outlook, we remain confident in our ability to drive improved performance and deliver sustainable growth.”

 

Updated 2026 Financial Outlook and Guidance:

 

●Full year 2026 revenue of $125 million to $130 million

 

●Full year 2026 gross profit as a percentage of revenue of 17 percent to 19 percent

 

The Company’s outlook assumes, among other things, continued access to its asset-based credit facility and no material further changes in tariff policy, and is subject to the risks described below under “Cautionary Statement Regarding Forward-Looking Statements.

 

2026 Second Quarter and Recent Events:

 

●Announced the addition of the XREAL xbx a01+, a lightweight pair of augmented reality (“AR”) screen-casting glasses, to Drone Nerds’ product lineup

 

 

 

●Entered a partnership with DBOX to expand access to autonomous drone docking solutions across the U.S. public safety and government markets through Drone Nerds’ enterprise solutions portfolio

 

●Dr. Allan Evans appointed as Chairman of the Board of Directors, and Jeremy Schneiderman appointed as Interim CEO, further enhancing the Company’s expertise in the U.S. enterprise drone market

 

●Completion of the internal review of matters relating to the Company’s former Chief Executive Officer and related corporate governance matters by a committee of independent directors, assisted by independent counsel, concluding that no financial misstatements were identified.

 

Liquidity and Reduction of Corporate Expenses

 

As of June 30, 2026, the Company had $7.2 million of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and $9.1 million of remaining availability on the borrowing base under its credit facility at Drone Nerds.

 

During the second quarter the Company took significant action to reduce its corporate and ADS headcount and related compensation, to consolidate its office footprint, and to terminate non-core consulting, vendor and software contracts. On a preliminary basis, average monthly operating cash outflows of the Company’s ADS and corporate operations (excluding Drone Nerds) during the third quarter were approximately $1.1 million per month, down approximately 42% from the second quarter, and 62% from the first quarter.

 

As of September 30, 2026 on a preliminary basis the Company had $5.1 million of unrestricted cash and cash equivalents. The Company continues to reduce its monthly cash burn, resulting in an expected transition to positive net cash provided by operating activities during the fourth quarter of 2026.

About XTI Aerospace, Inc.

 

XTI Aerospace, Inc. (“XTI”) (Nasdaq: XTIA) is focused on building a leading platform for commercial and government drone adoption in the growing unmanned aircraft systems (“UAS”) industry. Through its subsidiary, Drone Nerds, LLC, XTI supports enterprise and government customers in sectors including public safety, agriculture, construction, and energy across the drone program lifecycle, from hardware sourcing and deployment to training, service, repair, and the proprietary Always Flying™ program. Its established U.S. customer base and reputation as a trusted solutions provider position the company as a go-to-market partner for drone manufacturers and software developers seeking to scale in the domestic market.

 

For more information about XTI, please visit xtiaerospace.com and follow XTI on LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and YouTube.
