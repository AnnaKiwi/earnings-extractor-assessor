Tenax Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

Completed Statistical Analyses of LEVEL Yielded a Predictive Enrichment Strategy Being Applied in Amended LEVEL-2 Protocol

Company Has Been Granted a Type C Meeting with the FDA

CHAPEL HILL, N.C., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Tenax Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: TENX) (“Tenax” or “Tenax Therapeutics” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided an update on recent corporate progress.

On August 10, 2026, Tenax announced that LEVEL, the first Phase 3 clinical trial of oral levosimendan in patients with pulmonary hypertension associated with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (PH-HFpEF), did not achieve its primary or key secondary objectives, but did demonstrate evidence of beneficial biological effects on predefined cardiac biomarker and pulmonary hemodynamic measures in the overall population. Clinically meaningful improvements were observed in cardiac wall stress as measured by NT-proBNP (49% reduction, nominal p<0.0001) and in right ventricular systolic pressure (RVSP), an echocardiographic measure of pulmonary arterial pressure (reduction of 3.5 mmHg, nominal p=0.0045). Subgroup analyses identified a meaningful beneficial treatment effect in patients with the most advanced disease, defined by their limitation in exercise capacity, hemodynamic elevation in central venous pressure (CVP), pulmonary arterial pressure (PAP), and pulmonary capillary wedge pressure (PCWP), and elevated NT-proBNP levels. The treatment effect in the half of the LEVEL population with greater exercise impairment was manifested not only by clinically meaningful changes in 6-minute walk distance (6MWD), but also by greater improvement in pulmonary hemodynamic measures than was observed overall.

According to Stuart Rich, Chief Medical Officer of Tenax Therapeutics, “NT-proBNP and measures of PAP such as RVSP are independent biomarkers used in everyday diagnosis and treatment of patients with all forms of heart failure and pulmonary hypertension. Decades of research link improvements in both markers to reductions in mortality in patients suffering from these diseases. We believe the combination of robust reductions in both NT-proBNP and PAP in the patients treated with levosimendan support the hypothesis that levosimendan will provide meaningful chronic clinical effectiveness.”

On August 29, 2026, in a Late-Breaking Clinical Science session at the European Society of Cardiology (ESC) Congress, Professor Sanjiv Shah of Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine, who serves as LEVEL’s Principal Investigator, reviewed in more detail a prespecified analysis of the LEVEL population: in patients who walked less than the trial median of 333 meters at baseline, the levosimendan group demonstrated a 26.3 meter improvement in 6MWD as compared to the placebo group, a strong drug-treatment benefit in a more characteristic PH-HFpEF population. In this group of more symptomatic patients who demonstrated impressive exercise improvement, the observed RVSP reduction was greater than in the overall trial population (4.9 mmHg reduction, nominal p=0.009).

 

On October 8, 2026, the Company disclosed the completion of a multivariable analysis of LEVEL results that identified baseline characteristics as predictors of enhanced treatment response, described a predictive enrichment strategy for LEVEL-2 resulting from this analysis, and announced a temporary pause on LEVEL-2 enrollment while a protocol amendment is implemented at each site to ensure the population recruited for the trial meets these requirements. The enrichment criteria for LEVEL-2 were derived from a post hoc predictive enrichment analysis of the LEVEL data using a statistical methodology that estimates individual-level treatment effects using arm-specific regression models and then applies classification trees to identify baseline characteristics that distinguish treatment-responsive from non-responsive patients.

“Analysis of the results of the LEVEL trial provides insights into those patients who exhibit a significant improvement in exercise tolerance. With treatment continuing for those randomized prior to October, we believe we have now taken the steps necessary to ensure our LEVEL-2 protocol population will be enriched with these more symptomatic patients,” said Chris Giordano, President and Chief Executive Officer of Tenax Therapeutics. “In a LEVEL population with greater exercise limitation at baseline (i.e., lower 6MWD) and more pronounced echocardiographic evidence of volume overload causing their left heart disease, an improvement of 34 meters in 6MWD was observed after 12 weeks in this clinical trial. Our objective in LEVEL-2 is to enroll a population that conforms to this LEVEL subpopulation demonstrating significant treatment benefit on levosimendan, which made up more than 50% of patients in LEVEL, following the protocol amendment we are in the process of implementing.”

Mr. Giordano added, “We shared the topline LEVEL data with the Food and Drug Administration (“FDA” or the “Agency”) in mid-August, have updated the Agency on these hypothesis-generating observations from LEVEL and have been granted a Type C meeting to discuss the overall development of levosimendan in PH-HFpEF. We remain focused on our mission to bring the first approved therapy to these patients.”

Recent Corporate and Clinical Highlights

 

  •  

In August 2026, Tenax announced topline results from the Phase 3 LEVEL clinical trial of TNX-103 (oral levosimendan) in patients with PH-HFpEF. LEVEL did not meet its primary endpoint of improvement in 6MWD versus placebo. However, in a prespecified analysis, patients with a baseline 6MWD below the trial median of 333 meters showed a 26.3-meter improvement compared to placebo (95% CI 6.0, 46.7; nominal p=0.0112).

 

  •  

In prespecified exploratory analyses in the overall trial population, treatment with TNX-103 resulted in a 49% greater reduction in NT-proBNP compared to placebo (nominal p<0.0001) and a 3.5 mmHg reduction in RVSP compared to placebo (nominal p=0.0045).

 

  •  

TNX-103 was generally safe and well tolerated, with serious adverse events and adjudicated clinical worsening events balanced across treatment groups.

 

  •  

In August 2026, LEVEL results were presented by Sanjiv Shah, MD, Director of the HFpEF Program at Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine and LEVEL’s Principal Investigator, in a Late-Breaking Clinical Science session at the ESC Congress 2026 in Munich, Germany.

 

  •  

Approximately 20 echocardiographic parameters improved with TNX-103 compared to placebo, and no significant arrhythmias were observed across the overall trial population.

 

  •  

With full statistical analysis now complete, the Company confirms TNX-103’s biological effects include marked reductions in PAP and NT-proBNP, a biomarker reflecting increased cardiac wall stress.

 

  •  

Based on multivariable analyses of LEVEL, Tenax has temporarily paused enrollment and is amending the protocol for LEVEL-2, its ongoing global Phase 3 clinical trial, to enrich for patients with either a baseline 6MWD of 100 to 300 meters, or a baseline 6MWD of 301 to 400 meters and a right atrial volume index (RAVI) of at least 25 mL/m².

 

  •  

In LEVEL patients meeting these criteria, TNX-103 showed a 34-meter improvement in 6MWD compared to placebo at Week 12 (95% CI 15, 53; nominal p=0.001).

 

  •  

Enrollment in LEVEL-2 has been temporarily paused while the amendment is implemented.

 

  •  

Tenax shared the topline LEVEL results with the FDA in mid-August and has been granted a Type C meeting to discuss the overall development of levosimendan in PH-HFpEF.

 

  •  

Tenax expects to publish the LEVEL-2 protocol design paper and to provide further updates on the development of levosimendan in the first quarter of 2027.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Cash position: Tenax Therapeutics reported cash and cash equivalents of $113.7 million as of September 30, 2026. The Company utilized $4.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 but has $16.2 million more cash and cash equivalents than on December 31, 2025. Tenax now expects its cash runway to fund operations through fiscal 2028, but its current operating plan is subject to change pending the outcome of its Type C meeting with the FDA.

Research and development (R&D): R&D expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $12.1 million, compared to $10.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase is primarily attributable to increased preclinical development costs and clinical costs associated with the Company’s LEVEL and LEVEL-2 clinical trials.

Selling, General and administrative (SG&A): SG&A expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $5.7 million, compared to $6.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease is primarily attributable to a decrease in stock-based compensation expense, partially offset by an increase in professional fees as well as salary and benefit costs.

Net loss: Tenax Therapeutics reported a net loss of $16.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a net loss of $15.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

About Levosimendan (TNX-101, TNX-102, TNX-103)

Levosimendan is a novel, first-in-class K-ATP channel activator/calcium sensitizer currently being evaluated to treat pulmonary hypertension (PH) associated with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (PH-HFpEF). Levosimendan was first developed for intravenous use in hospitalized patients with acutely decompensated heart failure, and it has received market authorization in 60 countries in this indication, although it is not available in the United States or Canada. TNX-103 (oral levosimendan) is being evaluated in LEVEL-2, a Phase 3, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial in patients with PH-HFpEF.

About Tenax Therapeutics

Tenax Therapeutics, Inc. is a Phase 3, development-stage pharmaceutical company using clinical insights to develop novel cardiopulmonary therapies. The Company owns global rights to develop and commercialize levosimendan, which it is developing for the treatment of PH-HFpEF, the most prevalent form of pulmonary hypertension globally, for which no product has been approved to date. For more information, visit www.tenaxthera.com. Tenax Therapeutics’ common stock is listed on The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC under the symbol “TENX”.
