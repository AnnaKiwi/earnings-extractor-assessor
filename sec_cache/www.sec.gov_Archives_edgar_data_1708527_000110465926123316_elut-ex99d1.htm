Elutia Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results: As Plastic Surgeons Cite Infection Risk in Every Breast Research Session During National Meeting, NXT-41x Heads to FDA This Quarter, Fully Funded Through Launch

●Infection is a Major Unmet Need in Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery: Infection risk was cited in all 11 breast research sessions at the American Society of Plastic Surgeons’ annual meeting, with serious infection rates reported as high as 23%.

​

●NXT-41x In Vivo Study Supports Planned Q4 2026 FDA Submission: The GLP rabbit antibacterial study achieved its prespecified performance endpoints, with no viable bacteria recovered after either the 8-log MRSA challenges or the 6-log E. coli challenges, demonstrating robust eradication of relevant pathogens.

​

●$26 Million in Capital Received: Elutia added $26 million without an equity offering, which is expected to fund the company through the anticipated clearance and first full-year of NXT-41x commercialization in 2028.
●Cardiovascular Revenue Grows 20%: Elutia's cardiovascular business delivered 20% year-over-year revenue growth in the third quarter of 2026 and 65% growth for the first nine months of 2026.

​

GAITHERSBURG, Md., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Elutia Inc. (Nasdaq: ELUT) (“Elutia” or the “Company”), a drug delivery company built to stop surgical infections, today provided a business update and announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Patients and surgeons have been ignored for too long,” said Dr. Randy Mills, Chief Executive Officer of Elutia. “Walking the ASPS annual meeting in Houston, I was astonished by the disconnect between what surgeons are worried about and what industry is offering. Infection came up in all 11 breast research sessions, with rates reported as high as 23%. Those numbers tell the story of an unacceptable problem and the definition of a serious unmet medical need.

“We are out to solve it, and will take the next step this quarter with the NXT-41x FDA submission. After adding $26 million without an equity offering, we believe we have the technology, the team, and the capital to advance NXT-41x toward anticipated clearance in the first half of 2027 and through the first full year of commercial launch in 2028. In doing so, we will help patients thrive without compromise.”

​

Infection Has Become a Major Focus at ASPS. No Commercial Product Addresses It.

At Plastic Surgery The Meeting 2026 (PSTM 2026), the annual meeting of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons held October 15 to 18 in Houston, the program highlighted a major gap between the prevalence and severity of surgical infections and the lack of products to address them.

Infection risk and prevention were discussed in all 11 breast research sessions, and 70 of 135 (51.9%) oral research presentations addressed infection.

Infection is common despite current best practices. Independent investigators presented data at the meeting showing:

​

●In a study of 1,094 breasts undergoing immediate implant-based surgery, infection rates were 17.6% in women who did not receive chemotherapy before surgery and 23.0% in those who did (Lopes A, et al., PSTM 2026).
●Another study of 419 breasts treated with perioperative and postoperative antibiotics found infection rates of 19.3% (Fernandez K et al., PSTM 2026).

More systemic antibiotics do not help. In a multicenter study of 4,647 patients at 170 healthcare organizations, extending antibiotics past 24 hours did not reduce infection at 30, 60 or 90 days (Yadav K, et al., PSTM 2026).

The gap on the floor. Despite the intense focus on infection across the scientific program, no commercially available product on the exhibit floor addressed infection in breast surgery. Elutia is developing NXT-41x to address this gap and deliver localized antibiotic therapy directly to the surgical site in reconstructive surgery.

​

NXT-41x Heads to FDA This Quarter

The FDA review of NXT-41 (biologic surgical matrix without drug) remains on track, with a clearance decision anticipated this quarter. Elutia also intends to submit NXT-41x, its leading drug delivery program, to FDA in the fourth quarter of 2026, following positive results from a key performance study supporting the submission.

NXT-41x demonstrates potent antibacterial activity. NXT-41x is a biologic matrix with an antibiotic coating on one side only. In a GLP rabbit study of infection, the bacterial challenge was applied directly to the uncoated side, a worst-case test. NXT-41x met its predetermined performance endpoints, achieving complete eradication of an 8-log MRSA and a 6-log E. coli challenge, with no viable bacteria recovered from either the matrix or the surrounding tissue. By contrast, 100% of animals implanted with a matrix without antibiotics developed heavy colonization by the offending pathogens. MRSA, short for methicillin-resistant Staphylococcus aureus, is a highly drug-resistant form of the most common pathogen in breast surgery, making these results especially relevant to surgeons and patients.

Surgeons back the approach. At PSTM 2026, Elutia held an in-person meeting of its NXT-41x Surgeon Steering Committee, which provided very positive feedback on the design and performance of NXT-41x. Surgeons were clear: “It’s about the drugs being in the right place at the right time.” For NXT-41x, the right place means powerful antibiotics uniformly distributed across the entire surgical site, where systemic antibiotics fail to reach. The right time means 30 days of coverage, through drain removal.

Built for launch. Elutia’s automated drug-coating system is positioned for commercial production, with at least $300 million in revenue capacity, and is designed to support gross margins above 80% at scale.

​

Funding Milestones Achieved

Elutia delivered all three funding milestones it named in August, on schedule:

●October 1: Boston Scientific released the full $8 million held in escrow from the 2025 sale of the BioEnvelope business, with no claims.
●August 17: Elutia closed the sale of its SimpliDerm business to Cellution Biologics Inc., receiving $8 million at closing. Elutia is also eligible to receive up to $3 million in contingent technology transfer and commercial milestone payments over 18 months.
●August 11: Elutia closed a $15 million loan facility with Avenue Capital Group, with $10 million funded at closing and an additional $5 million available following NXT-41x FDA clearance.

Collectively, these transactions added $26 million in 51 days, without an equity offering. The Company believes it has sufficient funding through NXT-41x clearance and launch. Upside to that runway includes the additional $5

​

million from Avenue Capital, up to $3 million in SimpliDerm milestone payments, and potential proceeds from the sale of the Cardiovascular business.

Cardiovascular sales grew 20% to $1.1 million in the third quarter, up from $0.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, and increased 65% for the first nine months, with a third quarter adjusted gross margin (non-GAAP) of 85%. The strategic process for the business remains on track for 2026. As the business grows, Elutia is considering a transaction only on terms that reflect its value. Elutia’s current cash runway through the anticipated first full year of NXT-41x commercialization does not depend on a Cardiovascular transaction.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Net sales and operating results discussed below reflect continuing operations. For the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same period of 2025:

●Net sales were $1.1 million, compared to $0.9 million, an increase of 20.4%.
●Gross margin on a GAAP basis was 61.7%, compared to 52.8%.
●Adjusted gross margin (a non-GAAP measure which excludes non-cash amortization of intangibles) was 85.5%, compared to 81.3%. A reconciliation of GAAP gross margin to adjusted gross margin is included in the accompanying financial tables.
●Total operating expenses were $7.6 million, compared to $6.4 million. The increase was driven by a $1.9 million increase in research and development expense supporting the NXT-41 and NXT-41x programs, partially offset by a $0.7 million reduction in net litigation costs.
●Loss from operations was $6.9 million, compared to $5.9 million.
●Net loss from continuing operations was $6.5 million, compared to $1.0 million.
●Income from discontinued operations was $5.2 million, compared to a loss of $2.8 million.
●Net loss was $1.3 million, compared to $3.9 million.
●Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure that excludes from net loss certain non-operating, non-cash and non-recurring items) was a loss of $5.6 million, compared to a loss of $3.4 million. A reconciliation of net loss to adjusted EBITDA is included in the accompanying financial tables.
●Cash and cash equivalents at September 30, 2026 were $29.1 million. On October 1, 2026, the Company received the $8 million Boston Scientific holdback, which is not included in this balance.

About Elutia

Elutia Inc. (Nasdaq: ELUT) is a drug delivery company built to stop surgical infections. The Company developed and launched EluPro, the first antibiotic-eluting bioenvelope designed to protect patients receiving pacemakers and defibrillators, and in 2025 sold that business to Boston Scientific Corporation. Elutia’s lead program, NXT-41x, builds on the same drug-eluting platform and is designed to deliver two powerful antibiotics directly to the surgical site, stopping infection before it starts. Elutia’s mission is humanizing medicine so patients can thrive without compromise. For more information, visit www.Elutia.com.

Non-GAAP Disclosure

In addition to the Company’s financial results determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP, the Company provides non-GAAP measures that it determines to be useful in evaluating its operating performance and liquidity. The Company presents in this press release the following non-GAAP financial measures: earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization ("EBITDA"), adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization ("adjusted EBITDA"), adjusted gross margin and adjusted gross profit. The Company defines EBITDA as GAAP net loss excluding interest expense, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, and the Company defines adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA excluding income or loss from discontinued operations, stock-based compensation, FiberCel and VBM litigation costs, loss or gain on revaluation of warrant and derivative liabilities, warrant issuance expenses and loss or gain on revaluation of revenue interest obligation. The Company defines adjusted gross profit and adjusted gross margin as GAAP gross profit and GAAP gross margin, respectively, excluding amortization of acquired intangible assets. The amortization of these intangible assets will recur in future periods until such intangible assets have been fully amortized. Management believes that presentation of non-GAAP financial measures provides useful supplemental information to investors and facilitates the analysis of the Company’s core operating results and comparison of operating results across reporting periods. The Company uses this non-GAAP financial information to establish budgets, manage the Company’s business, and set incentive and compensation arrangements. Non-GAAP financial information, when taken collectively, may be helpful to investors because it provides consistency and comparability with past financial performance. However, non-GAAP financial information is presented for supplemental information purposes only, has limitations as an analytical tool and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. For a reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures to GAAP, see below "Non-GAAP Reconciliations of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA" and "Non-GAAP Reconciliations of Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin."
