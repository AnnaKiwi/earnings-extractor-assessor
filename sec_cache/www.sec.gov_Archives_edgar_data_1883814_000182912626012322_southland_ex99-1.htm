Southland Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

GRAPEVINE, Texas, November 12, 2026 – Southland Holdings, Inc. (NYSE American: SLND and SLND WS) (“Southland”), a leading provider of specialized infrastructure construction services, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

  ● Revenue of $233.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $213.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

 

  ● Gross profit of $20.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.9 million in gross profit for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

 

  ● Gross profit margin of 8.8% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to 0.9% gross profit margin for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

 

  ● Other income, net of $32.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.0 million in other income, net for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily driven by $28.7 million of other income related to the previously disclosed settlement with Liberty Mutual in connection with the Washington State Convention Center Project.

 

  ● Net income attributable to stockholders of $33.7 million, or $0.62 per diluted share for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss attributable to stockholders of $75.3 million, or $(1.39) per share for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

 

  ● EBITDA of $43.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $(3.5) million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.(1)

 

  ● Backlog of $1.71 billion.(1)

 

 

 

(1)Please refer to “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” and reconciliations for our non-GAAP financial measures, including “EBITDA” and “Backlog”

 

“Our third quarter results reflect continued execution of our strategic plan and an improvement in our operating performance,” said Frank Renda, Southland’s President & Chief Executive Officer. “During the quarter, we improved gross margin to 8.8%, delivered operating income of $7.0 million, and grew our backlog to $1.71 billion. This marks meaningful progress, and we remain focused on the work ahead. Demand for our services continues to be strong, and we believe Southland is well positioned to convert its share of new opportunities into profitable awards.”

About Southland

 

Southland is a leading provider of specialized infrastructure construction services. With roots dating back to 1900, Southland and its subsidiaries form one of the largest infrastructure construction companies in North America, with experience throughout the world. The company serves the bridges, tunneling, communications, data centers, transportation and facilities, marine, steel structures, water and wastewater treatment, and water pipeline end markets. Southland is headquartered in Grapevine, Texas.

 

For more information, please visit Southland’s website at southlandholdings.com.
