First Hawaiian, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Declares Dividend

HONOLULU, Hawaii October 23, 2026--(Globe Newswire)--First Hawaiian, Inc. (NASDAQ:FHB), (“First Hawaiian” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for its quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“I’m pleased with the solid earnings we delivered this quarter and the continued trust our customers place in us,” said Bob Harrison, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our deposit growth in a competitive market reflects the strength of those relationships, and our balance sheet is well positioned to benefit from rising interest rates. As we prepare for the Tri Counties Bank acquisition, we’re focused on building on that foundation and the growth opportunities ahead.”

On October 21, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.26 per share. The dividend will be payable on November 27, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 16, 2026.

​

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:

●Net income of $63.7 million, or $0.52 per diluted share
●Total loans and leases decreased $115.1 million to $14.5 billion
●Total deposits increased $50.4 million to $20.2 billion
●Net interest margin increased 5 basis points to 3.30%
●Results include a $24.3 million noninterest expense and a $31.0 million income tax benefit from an agreement to settle tax liabilities resulting from the 2016 corporate reorganization
●Merger-related costs in the quarter were $6.7 million
●Recorded a $20.3 million provision for credit losses, driven by a $12.5 million partial charge-off related to a single CRE loan and other CRE loan downgrades
●Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.26 per share

​

Balance Sheet

Total assets were $23.8 billion at September 30, 2026 versus $23.6 billion at June 30, 2026.

Gross loans and leases were $14.5 billion as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of $115.1 million from $14.6 billion as of June 30, 2026.

Total deposits were $20.2 billion as of September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026.

Net Interest Income

Net interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $174.1 million, an increase of $3.1 million compared to $171.0 million for the prior quarter.

The net interest margin was 3.30% in the third quarter of 2026, 5 basis points higher than the prior quarter’s margin of 3.25%.

Provision Expense

During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, we recorded a $20.3 million provision for credit losses. In the quarter ended June 30, 2026, we recorded a $5.6 million provision for credit losses.

Noninterest Income

Noninterest income was $56.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, $4.2 million lower than noninterest income of $60.3 million in the prior quarter.

Noninterest Expense

Noninterest expense was $160.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, $29.6 million higher compared to noninterest expense of $130.4 million in the prior quarter.

Noninterest expense in the third quarter included $6.7 million of merger-related costs and a $24.3 million noninterest expense from an agreement to settle tax liabilities resulting from the 2016 corporate reorganization.

​

The efficiency ratio was 69.2% and 56.2% for the quarters ended September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, respectively.

Taxes

The effective tax rate was -27.8% and 22.9% for the quarters ended September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, respectively. The negative effective tax rate in the third quarter was due to a $31.0 million income tax benefit we recorded as a result of the agreement to settle tax liabilities resulting from the 2016 corporate reorganization.

Asset Quality

The allowance for credit losses was $164.4 million, or 1.14% of total loans and leases, as of September 30, 2026, compared to $168.1 million, or 1.15% of total loans and leases, as of June 30, 2026. The reserve for unfunded commitments was $39.7 million as of September 30, 2026 and $37.7 million as of June 30, 2026. Net charge-offs were $21.9 million, or 0.60% of average loans and leases on an annualized basis, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to net charge-offs of $4.1 million, or 0.11% of average loans and leases on an annualized basis, for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Total non-performing assets were $46.0 million, or 0.32% of total loans and leases and other real estate owned, on September 30, 2026, compared to total non-performing assets of $39.5 million, or 0.27% of total loans and leases and other real estate owned, on June 30, 2026.

Capital

Total stockholders' equity was $2.8 billion at September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026.

The tier 1 leverage, common equity tier 1 and total capital ratios were 9.61%, 13.50% and 14.74%, respectively, on September 30, 2026, compared with 9.46%, 13.27% and 14.52%, respectively, on June 30, 2026.

The Company did not repurchase any shares in the third quarter.

First Hawaiian, Inc.

First Hawaiian, Inc. (NASDAQ:FHB) is a bank holding company headquartered in Honolulu, Hawaii. Its principal subsidiary, First Hawaiian Bank, founded in 1858 under the name Bishop & Company, is Hawaii’s oldest and largest financial institution with branch locations throughout Hawaii, Guam and Saipan. The company offers a comprehensive suite of banking services to consumer and commercial customers including deposit products, loans, wealth management, insurance, trust, retirement planning, credit card and merchant processing services. Customers may also access their accounts through ATMs, online and mobile banking channels. For more information about First Hawaiian, Inc., visit the Company’s website, www.fhb.com.
