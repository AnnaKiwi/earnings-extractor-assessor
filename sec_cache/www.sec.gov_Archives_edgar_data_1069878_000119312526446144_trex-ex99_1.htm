TREX COMPANY REPORTS Strong Third QUARTER 2026 RESULTS, DELIVERING BACK-TO-BACK RECORD QUARTERS

 

 

 

26% Net Sales Growth Drives Record High Third Quarter Revenue

Sales Driven by Broad Strength Led by Wood Conversion Focus

Successfully Executing Enhanced Distribution Strategy

Arkansas on Schedule to Operate at 50% Capacity by Year-End

Raising 2026 Guidance on Strong Execution and Positive Outlook

 

Third Quarter Financial Highlights

 

•
Net sales of $360 million
•
Gross margin of 36.0% and adjusted gross margin of 36.9%
•
Net income of $52 million and diluted earnings per share of $0.52
•
Adjusted net income of $56 million
•
Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.55
•
Adjusted EBITDA of $100 million

 

WINCHESTER, Va. – November 4, 2026 – Trex Company, Inc. [NYSE:TREX], the world’s largest manufacturer of wood-alternative composite decking and railing, and a leader in high-performance, low-maintenance outdoor living products, today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

 

“We delivered an exceptional third quarter as we successfully executed our enhanced distribution strategy, began the ramp-up of our Arkansas manufacturing facility, generated record third quarter revenue, and again increased our full-year outlook,” said Adam Zambanini, President and CEO.

“Growth reflected strong underlying customer demand with additional benefit from distribution-related inventory investments. Our growth remains overwhelmingly volume-driven, with minimal benefit from pricing.

 

 

 

 

“We believe our underlying performance was driven primarily by Trex-specific initiatives and continued market share gains rather than any meaningful improvement in broader end-market conditions. Demand remained strong across all levels of decking products, demonstrating the effectiveness of our wood conversion strategy and our ability to attract a broader range of homeowners into the Trex brand.

“Our investments in marketing, innovation, and brand development continue to strengthen demand for Trex products and support our long-term growth objectives. These efforts, combined with our category leadership and expanded market reach, provide a strong foundation to continue gaining market share and delivering long-term growth across a range of market environments.

“The enhanced distribution strategy announced last quarter was executed successfully and remains on schedule. During the quarter, we completed inventory placements with our new and expanded distribution partners while inventory held by our former distribution partner continued to be reduced. Although inventory levels increased at selected distribution partners during the transition, overall channel inventory remained comparable to the prior year.

“Importantly, the transition was completed with minimal disruption across the channel and has strengthened our route-to-market capabilities. Together with our distribution partners, we believe we are now better positioned to support accelerated growth and capitalize on future market share opportunities.

“To support our long-term growth strategy, we continue to make excellent progress ramping production at our Arkansas facility. The facility remains on track to have approximately 50% of its existing decking production capacity operational by year-end. While startup costs were higher than anticipated during the quarter largely due to certain mechanical issues that have since been resolved, we continue to expect Arkansas to drive meaningful margin expansion over time through improved manufacturing efficiency, lower logistics costs, and closer proximity to key Sunbelt growth markets.

“The facility was also designed with future expansion in mind, allowing us to efficiently add production lines and increase capacity as demand grows. This provides Trex with substantial flexibility to support both near-term growth opportunities and our long-term objective of significantly expanding the business.

“Given our strong year-to-date performance, continued demand momentum, and confidence in our strategic initiatives, we are pleased to raise our full-year guidance once again,” Mr. Zambanini concluded.

 

Q3 2026 Financial Summary

 

All financial results comparisons made are against the prior-year period unless otherwise noted:

 

2

 

 

 

Net sales increased 26.0% to $360 million from $285 million, reflecting broad-based volume growth led by the Trex Enhance® product line, supported by strong consumer demand, continued success executing our wood conversion strategy, and ongoing market share gains. We estimate that approximately $30 million in revenue came from initial distribution load-in.

Gross profit was $129 million, with gross margin of 36.0%, compared to gross profit of $115 million and gross margin of 40.5% in the prior-year period. Excluding Arkansas plant startup costs, adjusted gross profit was $133 million, compared to adjusted gross profit of $117 million in the prior-year period.

Gross margin performance reflected higher transportation-related input costs associated with increased diesel prices, as well as higher depreciation resulting from recent capacity investments. Startup costs at the Arkansas facility were higher than anticipated due to a temporary mechanical issue that created operational inefficiencies during the quarter. The issue has been resolved, and the Company expects startup-related costs to moderate as production volumes increase as the facility advances toward full utilization.

Selling, general, and administrative expenses were $56 million, representing 15.7% of net sales, compared to $45 million, or 15.8% of net sales, in the prior-year period. Excluding digital transformation costs, SG&A was $55 million compared to $43 million in the prior year. The increase primarily reflects investments in people, capabilities, and brand-building initiatives designed to accelerate consumer demand and support future growth.

Net income was $52 million, or $0.52 per diluted share, compared to net income of $52 million, or $0.48 per diluted share, in the prior year. Adjusted net income was $56 million, with adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.55, compared to adjusted net income of $55 million, with adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.51.

Adjusted EBITDA increased 10% to $100 million from $90 million in the prior-year period.

Free cash flow for the quarter was $135 million, reflecting strong operating performance, seasonal working capital benefits, and lower capital expenditures as construction of the Arkansas facility approaches completion.

During the quarter, the Company repurchased approximately 1.2 million shares for $55 million and repaid $83 million outstanding under its revolving credit facility. The Company anticipates completing its Board-authorized $150 million share repurchase program by year-end, bringing total share repurchases during 2026 to approximately $300 million.

About Trex Company

 

For more than 30 years, Trex Company [NYSE: TREX] has invented, reinvented and defined the composite decking category. Today, the company is the world’s #1 brand of sustainable, wood-alternative decking and railing, and a leader in high performance, low-maintenance outdoor living products. Boasting the industry’s strongest distribution network, Trex sells products through more than 6,700 retail outlets across six continents. Through strategic licensing agreements, the company offers a comprehensive outdoor living portfolio that includes deck drainage, flashing tapes, LED lighting, outdoor kitchen components, pergolas, spiral stairs, fencing, lattice, cornhole and outdoor furniture – all marketed under the Trex® brand. Based in Winchester, Va., Trex is proud to have been named America’s Most Trusted® Outdoor Decking^ for the past 6 years (2021-2026). The company also holds a place on Barron’s list of the 100 Most Sustainable U.S. Companies (2024 and 2025), was named one of America’s Most Responsible Companies 2024 by Newsweek, ranked as one of the 100 Best ESG Companies by Investor’s Business Daily, and named the Sustainable Brand Leader in the decking category by Green Builder Media for the 16th consecutive year. For more information, visit Trex.com.

 

^Trex received the highest numerical score in the proprietary Lifestory Research 2021-2026 America’s Most Trusted® Outdoor Decking studies. Study results are based on experiences and perceptions of people surveyed. Your experiences may vary. Visit www.lifestoryresearch.com.
