CapsoVision Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SARATOGA, Calif., November 12, 2026 - CapsoVision, Inc. (NASDAQ: CV), a commercial-stage medical technology company developing advanced imaging and AI-enabled capsule endoscopy solutions, today reported financial results and provided a business update for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Recent Highlights
•Reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $3.6 million, compared with $3.5 million in the third quarter 2025.
•CapsoCam Plus® used by more than 184,000 patients worldwide as of September 30, 2026, compared with 151,000 a year earlier.
•Received FDA clearance for AI Highlights™, the Company’s AI-assisted reading tool for CapsoCam Plus®, on September 25, 2026, followed by its U.S. commercial launch on November 2, 2026. Available for both adults and pediatric patients aged two and older, AI Highlights supports physician review through both local computer and cloud-based platforms. The Company expects the addition of AI Highlights to accelerate CapsoCam Plus sales growth, with the full commercial impact anticipated in 2027.
•Completed enrollment and clinical data collection for the second-generation CapsoCam Colon™ study, including physician review of capsule videos both with and without AI assistance. FDA 510(k) submission remains on track for year-end 2026.
•Continued expanding the clinical footprint of the CapsoCam UGI study for pancreatic cancer detection. Building on two active community-based clinical sites, the Company has executed a clinical trial agreement with a third site and is in various stages of contracting and feasibility discussions with more than a dozen larger medical centers with expertise in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), which the Company expects will help accelerate patient enrollment.
•Completed an $18 million registered direct offering with existing shareholders in September 2026, generating approximately $17.8 million in net proceeds.
•Raised approximately $1.7 million in net proceeds through the at-the-market offering program during the third quarter of 2026.
•Appointed David S. Shields, M.D., to the Board of Directors effective July 1, 2026.
Management Commentary
"The FDA clearance and subsequent U.S. launch of AI Highlights marks an important step forward for CapsoVision,” said Johnny Wang, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our four-camera, 360-degree panoramic capsule already provides physicians with a differentiated view of the small bowel. By combining that imaging capability with AI-assisted review, we believe we can reduce reading time, improve detection accuracy compared with manual review, and give physicians an additional level of diagnostic confidence. We believe the combination of panoramic imaging and FDA-cleared AI-assisted review, approved for use in patients two years and older is a meaningful differentiator for CapsoCam Plus.
At the same time, completing enrollment in our colon clinical study brings us closer to our planned FDA submission by year end, which we believe represents a substantially larger market opportunity. With the support of our existing shareholders through our recent financing, we are focused on translating these milestones into broader adoption and long-term growth,” concluded Wang.



Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenue was $3.6 million compared to $3.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Average selling price was stable year over year. The Company expects the addition of AI Highlights, launched on November 2, 2026, following FDA clearance, to accelerate CapsoCam Plus sales growth, with the full commercial impact anticipated in 2027.

Gross profit was $1.8 million, compared with $1.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin was 49%, compared to 54% in the third quarter of 2025. The decline primarily reflected higher material costs associated with increased flash memory prices, a one-time inventory obsolescence charge, and higher stock-based compensation expense.

Operating expenses decreased to $9.1 million, from $9.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting continued expense management while supporting clinical and development programs, including the CapsoCam Colon pivotal study. With the study now complete, the Company expects related clinical expenses to decline while it continues advancing its development pipeline.
Net loss was $7.3 million, or $0.15 per share, compared with a net loss of $7.9 million, or $0.17 per share, in the third quarter of 2025.
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $21.8 million as of September 30, 2026. During September, the Company completed an $18 million registered direct offering with existing shareholders, generating approximately $17.8 million in net proceeds. The Company also sold 276,446 shares through its at-the-market offering program during the quarter for approximately $1.7 million in net proceeds.

About CapsoVision
CapsoVision is a commercial-stage medical technology company focused on developing advanced imaging and AI-enabled solutions to transform the detection and screening of gastrointestinal diseases. Its flagship product, CapsoCam Plus®, is a wire-free, panoramic capsule endoscope that enables high-resolution visualization of the small bowel and supports cloud-based or direct capsule video review. The Company’s next pipeline product, CapsoCam Colon™ with enhanced AI, is designed to enable non-invasive colon imaging and polyp detection. With a proprietary platform targeted to expand across multiple GI indications, including esophageal and pancreatic disorders, CapsoVision is advancing a new era in capsule-based diagnostics. For more information on CapsoVision, please visit www.capsovision.com.
